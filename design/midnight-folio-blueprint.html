The card that interrupts the scroll

Every other shop card is light grey with a flat avatar. Midnight Folio is the only dark object on the page, so it reads as rare before anyone reads a word.

3 · Sound and touch

Coins, bells and embers

Every sound is made in the browser with Web Audio, so there are no files to download. Players can mute them with a button in the header. Press a sound to hear it.

MomentSoundHow it's madeHaptic pattern (ms)
Any button pressCoin clink: a small gold coin tapped on glass2,480 Hz + 3,710 Hz sines, 8 ms attack, 120 ms decay, slight pitch drop8
Correct answerTwo-note bell chimeC6 then G6 triangle tones 110 ms apart, with octave shimmer, 0.8 s decay12, 40, 12
Streak growsEmber swell with a low thumpBand-passed noise sweeping 300 → 2,400 Hz over 0.45 s, a 110 → 70 Hz thump, an E6 sparkle20, 30, 20, 30, 60
Unboxing shakeThree rising ticksSquare blips at 900, 1,120 and 1,340 Hz15 → 20 → 25
Unboxing revealThud and C–E–G–C bell arpeggio82 → 50 Hz sine thud, then four triangle bells 90 ms apart30, 60, 120

Haptics use the browser's vibration API. Android phones in Chrome feel them; iPhones don't allow websites to vibrate, so they simply skip it. Browsers only play sound after you've tapped something, which is why nothing plays until your first press.

Mythic

Rarity tier: Mythic

The top tier, above Pro-only gear. It's the only item that re-skins the whole app.

  • The seal: an octagon cut like a wax seal, struck in gold foil, with an embossed inner ring and “MYTHIC” in spaced capitals. A diagonal glint crosses it every 4 seconds.
  • Edition I marks it as the first Mythic, a collection that can grow.
  • Price: 3,000 coins. That's about three months of Pro's monthly coins, or a £4.99 coin pack, or a long run of duels. It's earnable, so it isn't pay-to-win.
  • No fake scarcity: no countdown timers or “only 3 left” pressure.
